Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Drift Currents operates driftcurrents.com. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

We do not collect your name, email, or personal contact information unless you voluntarily contact us. When you visit our site, certain information is collected automatically, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or link that brought you here
  • Browser type and approximate geographic location (city/region level)
  • Interactions such as clicks on links and outbound booking links

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the following third-party tools that set cookies on your device:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — tracks site traffic and visitor behavior to help us improve the site. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Tag Manager — manages the deployment of analytics and tracking scripts on this site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To understand how visitors use our site and improve the experience
  • To analyze interest in our properties and host services
  • To measure the performance of our content

Amazon Associates

Drift Currents participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program. Links on our Curated Items page are affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you purchase through them at no additional cost to you.

Third-Party Services

Our site links to third-party platforms including Airbnb, VRBO, and Amazon. When you click through to those sites, you are subject to their respective privacy policies. We do not control or have access to data collected by those platforms.

Data Retention

Analytics data collected via GA4 is retained per Google’s standard data retention settings. We do not store personal visitor data on our own servers.

Your Choices

You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking via Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at driftcurrents@gmail.com.
