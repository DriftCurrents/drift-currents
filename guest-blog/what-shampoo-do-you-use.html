The Shampoo & Conditioner Guests Always Ask About

August 2026 · 3 min read

← Back to Guest Blog

We get this question a lot.

Somewhere between check-in and checkout, guests take a shower. And something about the combination of the mountain air, the sound of the river, and a genuinely good shampoo makes people stop and look at the dispenser.

Then they message us.

“What’s that shampoo? The moisturizing one?”
“Where do you get your conditioner? My hair felt amazing.”
“Is that the same brand as the body wash?”

Yes. It’s Ginger Lily Farms. And yes, we’ll tell you exactly where to get it.

Why We Chose It

When we were setting up the cabins, we knew we didn’t want those little single-use bottles. Wasteful, expensive at scale, and they make the shower look like a hotel that’s trying too hard.

We wanted wall-mounted dispensers — clean, no clutter on the shelf. And we wanted what went inside them to actually work.

We tested a few brands. Ginger Lily Farms won.

The shampoo is genuinely moisturizing — not the kind of moisturizing that’s just marketing copy. The conditioner actually detangles. The body wash smells like you chose it on purpose. And it all comes in gallon sizes, which means we refill the dispensers once a month instead of restocking individual bottles every single turnover.

Guests with color-treated hair love it. Guests with curly hair love it. Guests who haven’t thought about their hair in years notice it anyway.

Where to Get It

We carry the full Ginger Lily Farms trio — Moisturizing Shampoo, Moisturizing Conditioner, and Nourishing Body Wash — all in 1-gallon sizes. You can find all three on our Curated Items page under Guest Amenities, linked directly to Amazon so you can order the same products we use.

Take It Home

The same products you used in the shower are available on Amazon in gallon sizes — the exact ones we stock the cabins with. You do not need to hunt for the right version. The links on our Curated Items page go directly to what we use.

It’s a small thing. But small things are the whole point.
