Your Dog Will Love It Here

August 2026 · 3 min read

← Back to Guest Blog

We are dog people. We welcome dogs and are often booked when our competitors are not. If you will not go somewhere your dog cannot go, we get it completely. That is exactly why we built these cabins the way we did.

Your dog is not an afterthought here. Here is exactly what is waiting for them when you arrive.

What We Have for Your Dog

  • Dog bowls with a silicone mat — stainless steel, BPA-free, with a high-edge spill mat so the floor stays dry. Ready to go when you walk in.
  • A welcome treat — we leave Milk-Bone MaroSnacks out for your dog. It costs almost nothing and gets mentioned in reviews more than you would think. Your dog will find them immediately.
  • Poop bags by the door — Earth Rated, because guests forget them constantly on cabin trips. They are there when you need them.

The Setting

Toccoa Currents sits along the Toccoa River in Suches, Georgia, deep in the North Georgia mountains. Our cabins have riverfront access and are surrounded by trails, woods, and open air. For a dog, it is about as good as it gets.

The trails do not require a leash. The river does not require an invitation. And the fire pit at night does not require anything except a good dog curled up nearby.

Take It Home
