Already running an STR?

Most existing hosts have the basics covered but are leaving money on the table. No Facebook ads. No Amazon affiliate page. No merch. No regional listing. We can plug in wherever you are.

Buy Equipment

We do not purchase equipment for you, but we know what works. Our curated items list covers every product category you will need to set up a compliant, guest-ready property. Browse Curated Items →

Property Setup

One-time installs before your first guest arrives — Moen Flo leak detector, Ring contact sensors on owner areas and windows, Ecobee thermostat setup. We install. We do not manage.

🔑

Account Setup

Get your Airbnb account built right from the start — host profile, payout setup, verification, and platform settings configured before your first booking arrives.

🏠

Listing Setup

Professional listing creation on Airbnb — titles, descriptions, amenities, and photo guidance that put your property in front of the right guests.

📅

Calendar Setup

Minimum nights, availability windows, and booking settings configured so your calendar works for you — not against you.

💬

Guest Messaging

Automated message templates from booking confirmation through checkout — so every guest gets the right information at the right time.

🗺

List on a Regional Site

Get your property featured on a Drift Currents regional site — SoFlo Currents, Golden Currents, and future destination brands. Direct booking traffic from a curated audience already looking in your market.

📘

Facebook Setup

A professional Facebook page for your STR — business profile, branding, and initial content setup so guests can find and follow your property.

📲

Facebook Ads

Targeted Facebook ad campaigns for your STR — we set up your Meta pixel, build your audience, and run ads that put your property in front of the right travelers. Your pixel data also powers retargeting across Drift Currents regional sites.

🖼

Marketing Materials

Design and order print materials for your property — rack cards, coffee mugs, coasters, and more, each with a QR code tied to UTM-tracked links so you know exactly what is driving traffic.

🛍

Printify Store Setup

We build and launch your Printify merch store — branded mugs, tumblers, coasters, and apparel your guests can order long after checkout. A passive revenue stream tied directly to your property brand.

🛍

Amazon Associate Setup

We get you approved for Amazon Associates and set up your first affiliate links — so every product recommendation your guests see can earn you a commission.

💰

Guest Item$ on Amazon

We build and host a guest items page on a Drift Currents regional site — your affiliate links, our traffic. Guests scan a QR code, shop what they loved at the cabin, and the commission goes straight to you. Flat monthly hosting fee.

🤝

Vendor Referrals

Need a photographer, electrician, plumber, or cleaner? We connect you with trusted vendors in your market. We make the intro — you handle the relationship.

📊

Analytics

Competitor analysis and performance tracking to understand how your listing is doing and where the gaps are.
