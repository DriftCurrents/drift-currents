What Affiliate Income Looks Like for STR Hosts

August 2026 · 5 min read

← Back to Host Blog

A guest stays at your cabin. They love the sheets. They love the coffee setup. They ask you what brand the drinkware is. That question is a purchase waiting to happen — and most hosts let it go unanswered or answer it once in a message thread that disappears.

Affiliate income is what happens when you stop letting that moment walk out the door.

What Affiliate Income Is The Guest Items Page Amazon Associates Where the Page Lives The QR Bridge

What Affiliate Income Is

Affiliate income works like this: you recommend a product using a special link. Someone clicks your link and buys. You earn a commission on the sale. The buyer pays the same price they would have paid anywhere else. The commission comes from the retailer, not the buyer.

For STR hosts, the products that earn commissions are the same products already in the cabin. The sheets. The coffee maker. The cast iron skillet. The outdoor chairs. The drinkware. Guests who loved their stay already love those products. The affiliate page just gives them a way to buy them.

The Guest Items Page

A guest items page is a curated list of everything in your cabin, organized and linked so a guest can find and buy any item with one click. It is not a store. It is a reference page — the answer to every “where did you get that?” question a guest has ever asked you.

The page works because the buyer is already warm. They did not discover this product on a random website. They used it, slept on it, cooked with it, or drank from it. The trust is built in. The only thing missing was the link.

Amazon Associates

Amazon Associates is Amazon’s affiliate program. It is the most practical affiliate program for STR hosts because most of the products in a cabin are already on Amazon. The approval process requires a website with real content. Commissions vary by category but add up across a full cabin’s worth of products with consistent traffic.

The program has requirements and rules that determine whether your links earn commissions. Understanding those rules before building your page matters — a page built incorrectly can result in account termination and lost earnings.

Where the Page Lives — And Why It Matters

A guest items page needs traffic to earn commissions. Traffic requires either a website with existing authority or a platform that already has visitors. A brand-new host website with no traffic produces minimal affiliate income regardless of how good the page is.

This is why where the page lives matters as much as what is on it. A page hosted on a regional STR platform that already has consistent traffic starts earning from day one rather than waiting for a new site to build an audience.

The QR Bridge

The physical connection between the stay and the affiliate page is a QR code inside the cabin. A guest who scans it during their stay — while they are surrounded by the products they love — is the most motivated buyer you will ever have. The QR code takes them directly to the page. One scan, and every product in the cabin is one click away.

That QR code is also trackable. You know how many guests scanned it, when, and which products they clicked. That data compounds over time and tells you what is working.
