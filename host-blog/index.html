Scout

Scout starts with a question most STR content skips: what is your goal? Not everyone is chasing ROI. Some hosts are covering carrying costs. Some are offsetting W2 income. Some got tired of renting someone else’s cabin. We bought where we wanted to vacation. Then we discovered the STR tax loophole — and that changed how we operate. Some decisions come from personal experience. Some from the host community. Some from cost of ownership. Some from personal values. Some from tax strategy. Know your goal first. Then know the rules. Then know your platform. And stay flexible — when the market shifts, the platform changes, the ordinance updates, or your own goals evolve, Scout tells you it is time to revisit a decision you thought was settled.
