STR Vendor Network

Vetted local vendors across every Drift Currents region. One directory. Every market. The trades, services, and professionals STR hosts need — curated so you do not have to find them cold.

Finding reliable vendors in an unfamiliar market is one of the hardest parts of launching an STR. The wrong cleaner costs you reviews. The wrong electrician costs you your permit. The Drift Currents vendor network exists to solve this. Every vendor listed has been vetted in their market. One or two per category. No cold calls.

Network listing: Vendors who list at the network tier appear across all active Drift Currents regional sites. Regional listing places you on a single market site. Contact us to discuss which tier fits your business.
