About Drift Currents

Drift Currents is a family-owned collection of short-term rental brands across the Southeast. We believe the best stays feel effortless — the kind where you arrive, exhale, and settle in. Every property we own or recommend meets that standard.

Beyond our own rentals, we help other hosts launch and grow their short-term rental businesses — sharing the systems, strategies, and hard-won lessons from our own experience.
